01 · Your invisible price

Strangers decide what you charge before they read a word.

I call it your invisible price. It's the number someone settles on in under a second, from your colours and the photo on your homepage, long before they reach your offers.

When your brand is doing its job, that number matches your real one. When it comes in lower, you spend the week making up the difference, answering enquiries that question your pricing and chasing proposals that stall for reasons you can't name. It gets into you as well. You hesitate before sending someone your link, and on calls you hear yourself quoting a little lower than you meant to.

Drift is usually what pulled it down. Somewhere between your first logo and your last price rise, the sharpest parts of you got softened out of your brand one sensible shortcut at a time: a caption ChatGPT finished for you here, a flyer approved on your phone between clients there. A new logo laid over the top drifts straight back.
