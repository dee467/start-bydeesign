Your business is moving fast. Your brand is struggling to keep up.

The algorithm wants more, and someone else is always about to ship the idea you've been sitting on, so you dive in before you're ready. You open Canva, ask ChatGPT for a caption, approve a flyer on your phone between clients and brief a freelancer in a two-line WhatsApp. Every tool you touch makes things faster, and none of them has the faintest idea who you are.

Each time you choose speed over consistency, your brand slips a little. One slip is fine, but they add up into drift, until one day you can't say what your brand looks like anymore or whether yesterday's post looks anything like last week's. You get swept up following trends and lose sight of what you wanted to be known for.

Meanwhile, strangers are deciding what you're worth. They do it in under a second, in rooms you're never in, and a drifting brand tells them a lower number than your work deserves.

When your brand loses itself, the right clients stop seeing themselves in it.

The brand manager in your pocket

Between our sessions, Deia does the job.

Is this for you?

This works best for founders with a business that's already running and selling, and a brand that hasn't kept pace.

This is for you if

  • You're getting enquiries, but from the wrong people: the ones who question your pricing, who found you but weren't quite looking for you.
  • You've outgrown your brand but don't know what to replace it with, so you keep shipping from the one you have and hope nobody notices the gap.
  • You know you should look more premium but you're not sure what that means for your brand specifically, so every decision gets made by gut feel.
  • You're making things in five different tools and none of it looks like it came from the same business.
  • You'd like to use AI to design, and you want to be the one steering it.

This is not for you if

  • Your business hasn't found its feet yet. The output is only as strong as the inputs you bring.
  • You want an AI to spit out a logo.
  • You want someone to design it all for you.
  • You want viral hacks or algorithm tricks.
  • You want a Pinterest board and someone to call it a brand strategy.
  • You want the whole brand finished by Friday. Each stage moves at the pace of your thinking, on purpose.

FAQ

Is this just a quiz?

It's the same strategy process I run with private clients, in conversation, with Deia reading every answer and pushing back when something doesn't add up.

Do I need design skills?

No. Every stage is guided, and Deia explains her reasoning as she goes, so you understand your brand as well as own it.

Do I have to design in Deia?

No. Use any tool you like. Deia and I are where you bring the result for review.

Will you design it for me?

No. You'll learn to direct it, which is why your brand stays yours.

What if I already have a logo?

Bring it. In Design, Deia checks it against your locked direction and tells you honestly whether it fits. She never generates logos with AI, and if yours isn't right she helps you get a mark that fits your brand, drawn properly.

Which tools does it work with?

Any of them. Deia gives you your brand in a form ChatGPT, Canva, Lovart or any site builder can work from, and checks what comes back.

I already use Claude. How is this different?

Deia is trained only on the DeBrand framework and asks the questions you didn't know needed answering. Claude is a brilliant general tool, and founders who get the most from it use both, with Deia setting the brand and keeping Claude on it.
