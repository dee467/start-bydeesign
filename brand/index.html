03Type

four fonts, four jobs

A condensed serif for most headlines, Lovelo capitals for the loud ones, Montserrat for everything small, and Sulu Script for the aside. If one of the brand fonts isn't installed on your computer, this page and the post maker use a close web stand-in, named below.

Times New Roman MT Condensed

your brand is in what you won't do

Headlines, the house style. Lowercase or sentence case, set big and tight. Stand-in: Instrument Serif.

Lovelo

Same look, same tool

All-caps headlines. Truths, questions and calls to action. Stand-in: League Spartan ExtraBold.

Montserrat

Sixteen years decoding brands.

Sub-copy, labels, pills, the handle. Bold for lines under a headline, regular for @deewoodward.

Sulu Script

things I won't do…

The aside. A short lead-in above a caps headline. Never a whole sentence. Stand-in: Sacramento.

House rules

The same rules Deia writes by.

Short

Short sentences. One point a slide.

Specific

If a line could be about anyone's brand, cut it or make it specific.

No em dashes

Use a comma, a full stop or "and".

British English

Plain words. No filler, no motivational phrases.
